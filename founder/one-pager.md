# MA Transport: one-pager

**Verdict: Not yet** (computed by the founder-plan tool)

**Problem.** Independent Australian charter and coach operators run quotes, bookings, vehicle and driver allocation, accreditation records and invoicing across spreadsheets, whiteboards and Xero, or older desktop systems. Double-bookings and expired driver authorities are costly. *(Industry pain is plausible but not yet quantified by any real operator.)*

**Customer.** Owner-managers of independent bus, coach and charter businesses with 3–30 vehicles, east coast first. The first audience is **growth-minded operators**, the only group that bought in the simulated panel.

**Offer and price.** "Clash-Free Charter Desk" founding-operator program: enquiry → quote → allocation → phone run sheet → Xero invoice, with clash and expiry blocking. Migration done for the operator, 60-day side-by-side run, 60-day money-back, data held in Australia with full export at any time. List price A$179 / A$379 / A$599 a month (5 / 15 / 30 vehicles), +A$15 per extra vehicle; founding operators get 30% off for 12 months; 10 places, closing 31 March 2027.
