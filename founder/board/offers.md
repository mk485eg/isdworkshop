# Offers lens
Score: 3 | Vote: PASS

## What is strong
- The buyers have money and a real job to get done. Charter and shuttle operators already pay for software: published Australian prices run from A$137.50 to A$385 a month (Coach Manager) and about A$215–240 plus setup (eCoachManager), and telematics costs A$25–35 per vehicle per month on top. So the habit of paying exists.
- The price is not the cheapest. Starter at A$199 sits inside the range other tools charge, so the business is not starting a race to the bottom.
- Accreditation and audits (NSW Passenger Transport Act, BOAS tool updated October 2025) give a clear, outside reason to keep good records. That is something to build an offer around.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **No proof of pain or likelihood.** There are no interviews, letters of intent or customers. A buyer has no reason to believe it works, and the brief has no evidence that operators find their current tools painful enough to switch. On the value equation, likelihood is close to zero today.
2. **It is a feature list, not an offer.** Fleet, drivers, bookings, scheduling, maintenance and reporting is what Coach Manager, eCoachManager, Busable and TransportMe already describe. The dream outcome is never named. With no outcome, guarantee or stack, the buyer only has price to compare, and on price it is middling.
3. **A small, hard-to-reach market with high switching effort.** IBISWorld counts 618 interurban/rural bus businesses; the brief has no data on how many are in the 5–30 bus target, how fast the market is growing, or how to reach them (no sales channel chosen). Moving bookings, drivers and maintenance history off an existing system costs the buyer a lot of time and effort.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Which operator, by fleet size and segment (charter, shuttle, school), loses the most money or sleep today, and how much? (Open question: no figure exists.)
- What do they use now, and what made the last one who switched leave it?
- What single result could you promise in 30 days, such as audit-ready records or fewer double-booked buses, and would you guarantee it?
- How many operators in the 5–30 bus range exist in Australia, and how will the first 10 hear of you? (Open question.)
- Who does the data migration, and how long does it take?
- If you charged A$399 for Starter, what would you have to add so it is still the obvious choice?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
Not applicable (PASS). What would move this to FUND IF:
- Ten or more operator conversations showing one specific, costly pain, with at least three written intents to pay.
- One named outcome with a guarantee (for example, done-for-you migration and audit-ready records within a set time, or money back).
- A stack that removes switching effort: data import, setup and staff training included.
- A chosen, tested channel to reach the target operators.
