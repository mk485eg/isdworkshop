# Product lens
Score: 3 | Vote: PASS

## What is strong
- The buyer is concrete: small and medium Australian bus, coach and shuttle operators. A product can be designed around one dispatcher's day.
- Fleet-size tiers (A$199 / A$399 / A$699) are simple to grasp, and dropping the per-trip fee removes a decision the buyer would otherwise face.
- Accreditation and audit duties (NSW Passenger Transport Act, BOAS audit tool updated October 2025) create a recurring moment where good records matter: a possible "one thing".

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **No one thing.** Fleet, drivers, bookings, customers, scheduling, allocation, tracking, maintenance and three kinds of reporting is a suite, not a product. Coach Manager, eCoachManager, Busable and TransportMe already cover parts of it, some at lower published prices (Coach Manager from A$137.50/month). A broad, unfinished suite loses to focused incumbents on every screen.
2. **No experience described.** No product, no interviews, no letters of intent, so nobody can say what the first five minutes feel like or which manual task disappears. The plan works forward from features, the opposite of what this lens asks.
3. **Seams with existing systems.** Tracking and maintenance overlap with telematics (Teletrac Navman, EROAD) and sometimes ticketing or school-bus tools. Each hand-off, including to accounting, is where data goes stale and trust breaks. No integration plan is named.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- In one sentence, what does this do for a 10-bus charter operator that nothing they use today does?
- If you could ship only one workflow (quote-to-booking, daily allocation, or audit-ready records), which, and why?
- Walk me through 7am Monday at a 15-bus depot: who opens the app, what do they see, what do they do?
- What would you cut so version one is excellent rather than complete?
- Which systems must it talk to on day one, and who owns a failure between them?
- How many operators have you watched work? (None are on file.)
- Who moves an operator's existing records in, and how long does it take?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
Not applicable at PASS. What would move the vote to FUND IF:
- One workflow described as a customer moment in one sentence, with everything else cut from version one.
- Operators observed or interviewed (how many is an open question) confirming that workflow is painful and badly served by current tools.
- A clickable prototype of it tested with those operators, plus a stated plan for GPS and accounting hand-offs.
