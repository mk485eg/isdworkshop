# Board brief: MA Transport

(The idea, exactly as the founder described it, plus the public facts gathered so far. Facts are labelled with where they come from; figures labelled [unverified] are the founder's own modelling assumptions.)

## The idea
- What it is: a cloud-based Transport Management System (TMS) for Australian passenger transport operators: fleet and bus records, driver management, bookings and customers, trip scheduling and allocation, trip status and tracking, maintenance and vehicle records, operational/business/financial reporting.
- Who it is for: private bus operators, coach charter companies, passenger transport and shuttle bus businesses, small and medium fleet operators in Australia. Larger operators (e.g. Kinetic, CDC, Busways, Transit Systems) named as a future market.
- Price (experimental, not validated): Starter A$199/month up to 5 buses; Growth A$399/month up to 15 buses; Fleet A$699/month up to 30 buses; extra buses about A$20–30/month. A per-trip fee was considered and set aside.
- Where/how: cloud web app sold online across Australia; international expansion later. Sales channel not decided.
- Stage: no product in market, no paying customers, no interviews or letters of intent on file. Budget not stated.
- Founder's earlier model [unverified]: a 15-bus operator, 13 active buses, 22 operating days a month, about 572–715 trips a month.

## Public facts found so far (from web search results; links in the founder's research)
- Coach Manager (Distinctive Systems), coach/charter operations software with Australian pricing: A$137.50/month (Express, level 1) to A$220 (Full, level 1), A$302.50 / A$385 (Express / Full, unlimited capacity), ex-GST; extra users A$27.50 each.
- eCoachManager markets coach/minibus hire software to Australian operators; listings show plans from about A$215–240/month plus setup fees of about A$300–350.
- TransportMe, an Australian ticketing/GPS/driver-compliance/maintenance platform, claims 150+ contracted companies and 3,500+ vehicles, and a Queensland government school-bus contract (vendor claim). Price not published.
- Other software in the space: Busable (Australian, regional operators), RouteStra, Moovs, Busify, Busie, CoachRail (mostly North American charter software, roughly US$50–199/month where published), school-bus tools (BusMinder, RollCall), enterprise scheduling (Trapeze, Optibus, GIRO HASTUS) used by large route operators, and telematics (Teletrac Navman about A$25–30/vehicle/month per aggregators; EROAD from $25–35/vehicle/month).
- Market: IBISWorld counts 618 businesses in Australian interurban and rural bus transport (which includes charter) in 2025, revenue about A$2.4bn. BusNSW says about 950 accredited bus operators in NSW. About 97,400 registered buses in Australia (Jan 2023, BITRE via Statista), many owned by non-transport organisations.
- Large operators run hundreds of buses each under government contracts (e.g. Kinetic Melbourne 602 buses, Transdev John Holland 472, CDC Melbourne 387, per Wikipedia).
- NSW requires bus operator accreditation (Passenger Transport Act 1990), with an audit regime (BOAS audit tool updated October 2025).
