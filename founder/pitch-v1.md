MA Transport: new cloud software for Australian bus, coach and charter operators, used in a web browser and on drivers' phones.
It keeps your bookings and customers, quotes, vehicle and driver allocation, trip status, vehicle maintenance records, driver records, and business and operational reports in one system.
Monthly subscription by fleet size, in Australian dollars: Starter A$199/month for up to 5 buses, Growth A$399/month for up to 15 buses, Fleet A$699/month for up to 30 buses; extra buses about A$20-30/month each.
It is a new product from a new company; there are no existing customers yet.
